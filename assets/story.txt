# run pack-story on this to make dist/story.chunk
#
#   :: name            starts a node, and the game begins at the first one
#   > label -> name    adds a choice
#   anything else      is prose, and a blank line is a paragraph break
#   a node with no choices is an ending

:: start
You are the captain of a submarine. You are eleven hundred feet down in the
Pacific, the engines are dead, and nobody knows where you are. Six hours ago
the machine that cleans the air stopped working.

The gauge is in the control room. You are the only one who has looked at it.
It says eleven hours.

You climb down the ladder. Eleven people stop what they are doing.

"How long?" the engineer says.

> Say eleven hours. -> said_truth
> Say sixteen hours. -> said_lie

:: said_truth
"Eleven hours."

Nobody argues with the number.

The engineer sits down against the wall and does not pick her tools back up.
You tell her to get up. She does not move. By the fourth hour you have stopped
asking.

> Continue -> hatch

:: said_lie
"Sixteen hours."

She nods and goes back to the air machine. By the third hour she has it in
pieces on the floor.

At the fourth hour she sits back and tells you it will not run again. You have
her keep working anyway, and she is still at it when the air goes bad.

> Continue -> hatch

:: hatch
Ten hours later. The air is down to about an hour and it is thick enough to
taste. You call everyone to the ladder.

There is one thing left to do and it is the hatch. At a hundred and fifty feet
a crew can go out through it and swim up. At eleven hundred feet the water
comes in like a wall and it is over in under a minute. Eleven people line up
and look at you.

> Say you would rather end it quickly. -> said_end
> Say we are swimming out. -> said_way_out

:: said_end
"Nobody is coming," you say. "There is an hour left and I am not going to make
you sit through it. I am opening the hatch. It will be quick."

Nobody says anything. The oldest of them shakes your hand on the way past.

> Turn the wheel -> ending_truth

:: said_way_out
"We are going out through the hatch and swimming up," you say. "Hold your
breath and do not stop on the way."

They have all trained for it. Not one of them says anything about the depth,
and you do not bring it up either.

Two of them push to the front of the line.

> Turn the wheel -> ending_lie

:: ending_truth
You turn the wheel. The hatch opens.

No water comes in.

You climb out onto the hull. There is a roof over you, a long way up, holding
the ocean off. Below the submarine there is a city, going down farther than
you can see. The others come up behind you. Somebody starts laughing and
cannot stop.

To be continued.

:: ending_lie
You turn the wheel. The hatch opens.

No water comes in.

You climb out onto the hull. There is a roof over you, a long way up, holding
the ocean off. Below the submarine there is a city, going down farther than
you can see.

Before you can get a closer look, someone puts their hands on your back and
shoves. You tumble down a cliff and die.

The end.
